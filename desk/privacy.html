Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

TheDesk app shows the day's picks, the results and the slate. You don't need an account to use it, and it doesn't ask who you are.

What we collect

Nothing personal to read the card. The app has no sign-up, no ads, no tracking scripts and no cookies. It never asks for your email, phone number or location. The one exception is the members' chat, below, and only if you choose to join it.

The members' chat

Anyone can read the chat. To post, you join with an invite code and pick a display name. To run it, we keep:

  • the display name you picked and the messages you post (the chat keeps the newest 400 messages; older ones are deleted automatically)
  • a pass on your device that keeps you signed in for 30 days
  • your IP address, for a few minutes only, to stop people guessing invite codes or flooding the room

The chat runs on Cloudflare. The owner can remove any message. To have your name and messages removed, email editor@ironsilkweekly.com.

What stays on your device

To open fast and work without a signal, the app keeps a few things in your browser's storage on your own device:

  • the last card and slate it loaded
  • whether you've seen the "put it on your home screen" guide

Nobody else can read it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Who your browser talks to

  • GitHub hosts the app and the published picks.
  • ESPN's public scoreboard supplies live scores while a game on the card is being played.
  • Cloudflare runs the members' chat.

Like any website visit, those services receive your IP address and basic browser details. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with them.

Our Facebook and Instagram app

TheDesk uses a Meta app, TheDesk Poster, for one job: publishing TheDesk's own posts (free picks, results and recaps) to TheDesk's own Facebook Page and Instagram account. It works only with accounts TheDesk owns.

  • It does not offer "Log in with Facebook" and never signs anyone in.
  • It does not read your profile, friends, photos, messages or anything else about you.
  • It reads only the reaction count on TheDesk's own posts, a single number, for goals like "100 reactions unlocks the full card". It does not record who reacted.
  • It stores no information about Facebook or Instagram users.

The same goes for the accounts we post to on Threads and X.

When you leave the app

The channel and the lounges are on Telegram, our pages are on Facebook, Instagram, Threads and X, and VIP checkout runs on Whop. Once you tap through, their policies apply. Whop handles your payment and account details; TheDesk never sees your card number.

Age

TheDesk is for adults 21 and over. It isn't meant for anyone younger.

The newspaper on this domain

IronSilk Weekly, the publication at ironsilkweekly.com, has reader accounts of its own. This page covers TheDesk app only.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: editor@ironsilkweekly.com.
