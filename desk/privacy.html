Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

TheDesk app shows the day's picks, the results and the slate. You don't need an account to use it, and it doesn't ask who you are.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app has no sign-up, no ads, no tracking scripts and no cookies. It never asks for your name, email, phone number or location.

What stays on your device

To open fast and work without a signal, the app keeps a few things in your browser's storage on your own device:

  • the last card and slate it loaded
  • whether you've seen the "put it on your home screen" guide

Nobody else can read it. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Who your browser talks to

  • GitHub hosts the app and the published picks.
  • ESPN's public scoreboard supplies live scores while a game on the card is being played.

Like any website visit, those services receive your IP address and basic browser details. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with them.

When you leave the app

The lounge and the channel are on Telegram, our page is on Facebook, and VIP checkout runs on Whop. Once you tap through, their policies apply. Whop handles your payment and account details; TheDesk never sees your card number.

Age

TheDesk is for adults 21 and over. It isn't meant for anyone younger.

The newspaper on this domain

IronSilk Weekly, the publication at ironsilkweekly.com, has reader accounts of its own. This page covers TheDesk app only.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: editor@ironsilkweekly.com.
